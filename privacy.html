Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Who we are

This is a small affiliate website that lists tech and home appliance products and links to them on Amazon. Contact: our contact email.

What we collect

  • Browsing without an account: your wishlist is stored only in your browser (localStorage) on your device.
  • If you sign in with Google: we store your name, email address, profile photo URL and wishlist in our database (Google Firebase), so your wishlist follows you between devices. We never receive your Google password.
  • Buy button clicks: when you press "Buy on Amazon" we record the product, its price, the commission rate and a timestamp. This record contains no name, email or IP address that we add.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data, we do not run advertising networks, and we do not use analytics or tracking scripts on this site.

Third parties

  • Google / Firebase provides sign-in and stores our data. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Amazon receives you when you follow a product link. Amazon may set its own cookies and tracks the referral so we can earn a commission. Amazon's privacy notice applies there.
  • Our web hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security and operation.

Your choices

You can clear your wishlist at any time by removing items, signing out, or clearing your browser data. To ask us to delete your account data, email us at our contact email.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13.

Changes
